Design Principles Applied

Grouped sections

White cards on light gray background. Each logical group in its own rounded rectangle. Section labels above each card.

Visual hierarchy

Hero card is largest and most prominent. Metadata rows are compact. Expandable sections use disclosure chevrons.

Consistent row style

Every metadata row: label left (secondary color), value right (primary). Separated by thin hairlines, not heavy dividers.

Breathing room

12px gap between cards. Section labels in uppercase small caps above each group. Generous internal padding.
